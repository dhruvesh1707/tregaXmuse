Support

Questions about an order, a payment, verification or anything else? Write to support@trega.in and we'll get back to you within 2 business days.

Refunds and disputes

For refund requests or disputes, raise the issue through Help & Support in the app within 48 hours of delivery (or of the failed pickup/delivery), and include your order ID.

Privacy requests

For access, correction or deletion of your personal data, write to support@trega.in with the subject "Privacy request". We respond within 30 days.

Feedback

Ideas to make Trega better are always welcome at support@trega.in.
